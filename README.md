Food Delivery App – Description

A Food Delivery App is a mobile or web application that allows users to order food from nearby restaurants and get it delivered to their location.

Main features:

User registration and login
Browse nearby restaurants
Search and select food items
Add items to cart
Online payment or Cash on Delivery
Order confirmation and tracking
Delivery status updates
Ratings and reviews
Order history
Offers and discounts

Simple description:
The app connects customers, restaurants, and delivery partners in one platform. Customers can easily choose food, place an order, make payment, and track the delivery until the food reaches their doorstep.
